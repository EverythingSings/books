Good, though not as good as *I, Robot*. There are pros and cons to having one investigation instead of several. It was cool to get into more character detail here, but the story felt a bit drawn out compared with the variety in *I, Robot*.

More character detail isn't always a good thing, though. Jessie, also called Jezebel, was annoying, and I didn't find her especially interesting. Maybe she was there to help drive the plot, and I can see the purpose in that. Not every character needs to be loved, so I don't necessarily see it as a fault in the writing. She was just an annoying character, and that stood out to me.

It also reminds me a lot of *Westworld*. I think we're probably going to see these kinds of themes play out in reality at some point.
