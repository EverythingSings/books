Incredibly good. The different investigative stories were varied enough to consistently keep my attention. I really enjoyed the way they explored the limitations of the laws of robotics. We get to see a bunch of different edge cases, and that was a big part of what made it interesting for me.

I'm also starting to realize just how much Isaac Asimov actually wrote. I definitely want to read more of his work.
